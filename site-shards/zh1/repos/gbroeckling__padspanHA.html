PadSpan™ HA

 See which room every phone, watch and tag is in, live, on a plan of your home



PadSpan is a Home Assistant integration for Bluetooth room presence. It listens to the Bluetooth proxies you already have, works out which room each phone, watch, tag and beacon is in, every 5 seconds, and shows it on a plan of your home: one floor at a time or all floors stacked. Your automations get room, distance and home/away sensors. The tracking runs locally.

▶️ Try it in your browser: padspan.traks.ca/demo, the real panel on a sample house. Nothing to install.

 Install: one click opens PadSpan in Settings → Marketplace (Home Assistant 2026.11 and newer) or in HACS (older versions). Or search either one for PadSpan.

What do you want PadSpan for?

- The whole house on one screen. Every light, door, lock, alarm and sensor on a plan of your home, each in its room. Tap a light, see what's open, and a leak or smoke alarm turns its room red on every screen until someone resets it. Put it on a wall tablet with the free kiosk link. Needs only Home Assistant 2024.8 or newer. See it
- Who's in which room. Every phone, watch and tag, room by room, on every floor, with a week of replay. Needs at least one Bluetooth scanner: an ESPHome Bluetooth proxy, a Shelly Gen2 or newer set as a Bluetooth scanner (one setting), your Home Assistant machine's own Bluetooth, or an ESPresense node. One tells you home or away; one in each room you want told apart gives you rooms. How many proxies do I need? Already running Bermuda? Keep it: PadSpan runs next to it. See it
- Your floor plan. Scan your rooms with an iPhone, start from your robot vacuum's map (experimental), or draw them, with split levels, room heights and lofts as they really are. Pro adds the house in 3D. See it
- Every light in its place. Free: every light on the map in its room, and a wall dimmer with its smart bulbs working as one light. Pro: each light exactly where it hangs, in its real shape. See it

Free and open source (GPL-3.0). PadSpan Pro adds exact placement on the Atlas, the touchscreen map of the whole house, plus Live Aboard (your house in 3D, like looking into a house in The Sims) and Furnish. Try all of Pro free for 90 days, no card. The anonymous usage report stays on during the trial (how it works).
