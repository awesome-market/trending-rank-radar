 What Jasna can do

- Restore mosaics in videos. Only the mosaic areas change. Quick start
- Pick fast or detailed. BasicVSR++ is fast and free; LTX is slower with more detail (supporters). Choosing models
- Test LTX for free. Trial mode shows how fast LTX runs on your PC. LTX options
- Use LTX only where it helps. Big mosaics get LTX, the rest BasicVSR++. Mosaic scanning
- Restore only some scenes. Choose ranges with a preview in the Segment Editor. Segment Editor
- Pause and continue. Long LTX jobs keep their progress. Pause and continue
- Sharpen restored areas. UNet 4x, RTX Super Res or Topaz. Secondary restoration
- Upscale the whole video to 1080p, 1440p or 4K. Upscale
- Smoother motion. Double the frame rate on RTX 40 and 50. Frame generation
- VR180 videos, side by side or top/bottom. VR180
- Still images with SD 1.5 (supporters). Images
- Watch while it restores, in the app or in a browser. Video Player, Streaming
- Many videos at once. Queue, presets and an action when the queue is done. Using the GUI
- Output extras. Color LUT, sharpening, 60→30 FPS, play while it's being made. Advanced processing
- Command line and rented GPUs. CLI, Cloud GPUs

 What you need

- NVIDIA GTX 16-series / RTX 20-series or newer, with a recent driver
  (610+ on Windows, 580+ on Linux). GTX 10-series and older don't work.
- Or AMD Radeon RX 6000 or newer (experimental). RX 7000/9000 are tested;
  RX 6000 is untested.
- A folder on a local disk, with no Chinese, Japanese, accented or other
  non-English characters in its path.


More details

- NVIDIA: your GPU needs compute capability 7.5 or higher in NVIDIA's
  GPU table. Data-center GPUs like
  A100, H100 and B200 don't work.
- AMD: see the table below. UNet 4x secondary restoration is supported.
- Disk format: NTFS on Windows; ext4, Btrfs, XFS or ZFS on Linux. Network disks
  are not supported. Use an SSD; on a hard disk (HDD) Jasna starts and loads
  models slowly.
- LTX needs an NVIDIA RTX or AMD Radeon RX 7000/9000 GPU (AMD integrated GPUs
  untested), at least 8 GB VRAM and 16 GB RAM; on Linux, kernel 5.8 or newer.
  See What LTX needs.
