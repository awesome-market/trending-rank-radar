SourceIO 6.0.0

Requires Blender 4.5 LTS or newer.

 New: Source 1 model export
Build Source 1 models in the new SourceIO model definition node editor and compile them with StudioMDL. Blender Source Tools is not required.
- Exports DMX or SMD geometry, QC, VMT materials and VTF textures. You can preview the export, export meshes or materials only, or export and compile in one step.
- Bodygroups, skins, LODs, collision, hitboxes, attachments (with empties you can move in the scene) and raw QC.
- Flex controllers, including stereo flexes, corrective rules and wrinkles.
