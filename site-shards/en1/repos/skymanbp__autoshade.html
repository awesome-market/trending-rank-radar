 [!TIP]
 The generated picture is a target, never the delivery. An image model paints the look you describe;
 AutoShade fits an editable develop recipe to it and renders that recipe on the full-resolution RAW.
 The recipe carries the look, the sensor carries the detail — and in the recipe path, the AI never touches a pixel.

Two AI paths, kept apart in the app, the command line and this page:

- AI generation & reverse-fit — an image model paints the picture you describe from your own frame (reimagine), and the reverse fit recovers that look as an editable recipe on the full RAW (match); one five-step strength control, Light to Full + AI review, sets how far the fit may go. The generated pixels are only ever a target.
- AI analysis & auto develop — a vision model looks at the photo and proposes the recipe directly (analyze, auto), guided by your reference library or a typed direction; when its own visual review finds that develop clearly improvable, the model states how the finished picture should measure instead, the reverse fit finds the recipe, and the review picks between the two; AI adjust sends the develop you are looking at with a request ("darken the sky a little") and gets the complete adjusted recipe back, with any part it names selected by a content mask cut locally.



 Highlights





🎯 Reverse-fit any look
reimagine generates the picture from your frame; match recovers a recipe from it — or from any finished rendition of the same shot. Look error 0.161 → 0.023.




🌌 A RAW denoiser trained here
Cleans the sensor mosaic before demosaic. Keeps 98.70 % of 17,817 faint stars on a real ISO 2500 frame.






📐 Every slider is a written law
Tone, Texture, Sharpening, masks and lens geometry are closed-form laws with named constants, each pinned by its own test.




🔁 Round-trips through Lightroom
An exported .xmp is merged byte for byte into Lightroom's own; the full develop survives a Lightroom rewrite and imports back exactly.






🖼️ Your library is the style guide
Point it at finished photos you love; each develop sees the 3 closest as a reference. Embeddings run locally.




🧩 Local AI masks, no API key
Subject, sky and point-prompted objects (BiRefNet, OneFormer, SAM 2.1), plus HDR, focus and noise stacking.
