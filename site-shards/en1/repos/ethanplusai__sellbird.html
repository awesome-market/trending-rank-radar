README

The open-source outreach platform that runs on your Claude or ChatGPT plan.


Sellbird finds companies that need what you sell, finds the person who decides, checks their email address, writes each one a personal cold email, sends it and handles the replies.
It runs on your own machine, and by default nothing goes out until you approve it.



  
  
  
  
  
  
  



  


 Harvey is now Sellbird. Same project, same history, new name. Already running Harvey? Your setup carries over: see Upgrading from Harvey.

New releases, features and the roadmap go out first on X: follow @sellbirdagent.

Prefer not to run it yourself? sellbird.ai is the hosted version, with sending domains, inboxes and warmup set up for you. It's in early access.

!Sellbird's dashboard: the Today screen, a conversation that needs you, approving a drafted reply in the Outbox, the signals to confirm, searching contacts, then dark mode

The demo data is invented: "Bookwell" selling online booking to roofers, dental practices and HVAC shops, on .test domains.

 What it does

Sellbird is a pipeline you can run end to end, or stop at any step.

| | What happens | More |
|---|---|---|
| Find companies | Searches for businesses that match your customer: free on OpenStreetMap, or on a paid source you pick. Every paid search shows its cost before it runs. | Discovery |
| Learn why they'd buy | Reads each company's own website for the buying signals you confirmed, like no online booking, ads running or a role they're hiring for. No model calls, no cost. | Signals |
| Find who decides | Finds the owner or manager named on the company's own pages, two people per company at most. | Enrich |
| Check the address | Learns the company's email pattern and verifies one address. Every address is marked verified, risky, guess or invalid, and only deliverable ones are ever sent to. | Verify |
| Write and send | Writes a short sequence for each person from what it found, then sends from your Gmail, Microsoft 365 or SMTP mailbox inside your daily limit and their office hours. | Sending |
| Handle replies | Reads every reply, drafts an answer, sets auto-replies aside, honours opt-outs at once, and hands you anything it shouldn't answer alone. | Dashboard |

Only want the list? Stop after verifying and run sellbird export for a sequencer-ready CSV of everything it found.
