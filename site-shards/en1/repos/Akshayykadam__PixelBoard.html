 🌟 What's New in v1.3.5

 🎙️ Gemini Rambler Natural Voice Dictation
- Screen Rotation Grace Period: Maintains active Rambler voice dictation across device display orientation changes by introducing a 1.5s grace period during display rotation, preventing sessions from abruptly aborting (146, PR 165 by Francisco Valentim Castilho).
- Preserve Speech Diff for Mid-Sentence Voice Edits: Retains speech diffs for voice replacements and mid-sentence voice commands (such as "replace X with Y"), word edits in respoken phrases, and mid-sentence cursor placements without overwriting surrounding compositions (167).
- Strict Language Enforcement & Anti-Drift: Disabled proactive parallel ASR and seamless fallback in Rambler stock policy to prevent voice dictation from unintentionally switching languages or drifting to similar locales (e.g., Czech &rarr; Slovak) (154).
- Eliminate Text Duplication on Dictation Start: Fixed an issue where starting dictation in an editor containing pre-existing text or recently pasted clipboard items would duplicate text at the cursor (166).
- Native Split Readiness Gate Check for Gboard 18.6.3: Added native library readiness gate verification on split components for Gboard 18.6.3 (Lsmi;, Locf;), ensuring smooth and stable JNI initialization on fused and split APKs (168).

 🤖 AI Writing Assistant
