Open Android DeX

 Your phone is already a computer. This gives it a desktop

Plug your Android phone into your Windows PC, Mac or Linux machine and a real
desktop opens on your screen, with a taskbar, an app drawer, and windows you can
drag, resize and snap side by side

Unplug it and your phone goes back to being a phone

Or install Open Android DeX directly on your phone and use it without a PC.
Run the launcher on the phone itself, on a compatible external monitor, or
access it through the built-in Web viewer in a browser.





Free. Open source. No account. No subscription. No root



  
    
    
  




---

 Top features

- Your phone becomes a computer when you plug it into your PC or Mac
- Open apps side by side and move or resize them, just like on a laptop
- Use any phone app on your computer, like WhatsApp, without opening the whole desktop
- Play your phone's games on your computer, with a controller, or with your keyboard and mouse
- Get your messages and calls on your computer, and reply or answer without picking up your phone
- Copy and paste between your phone and computer, and drag files onto your phone
- Send anything from your phone to your computer from the share menu, and see new photos there as you take them
- Use your phone as a touchpad to move the mouse and click
- Pull out the cable and keep working over Wi-Fi
- Works without a computer too, right on your phone or on a monitor
- Run Ubuntu Linux on your phone with Firefox and VS Code, plus Docker
- Open your phone's desktop in a web browser on any computer, with nothing to install
- Make it yours with themes, wallpapers, nine languages and a Windows 11 look
- Works on almost any Android phone with Android 8 or newer, not just Samsung
- Phones running postmarketOS work too: plug one in and its Plasma Mobile desktop opens in a window on your computer
- Your phone goes back to normal when you are done
- Free and open source, with no account, no subscription and no root

 Get started

 On a Windows PC, Mac or Linux computer

1. Download the app for Windows, Mac or Linux and install it
2. Turn on USB debugging on your phone. Go to Settings, then About phone,
   tap Build number seven times, go back, open Developer options and switch on
   USB debugging
3. Plug the phone in and tap Allow when it asks

The desktop opens on its own and the app gets out of your way. The desktop app
installs the phone-side launcher for you. Everything it needs comes inside the
download.

 The PC setup works with Android 8 or newer. You do not need a Samsung,
 a special cable, a monitor, or root.

 On Android, without a PC

1. Download OpenAndroidDeX-Launcher-v.apk from the
   latest release
   using your phone's browser
2. Open the APK and install it. If Android asks, allow your browser or file
   manager to install unknown apps
3. Launch Open Android DeX from your phone's app list. You can use it on the
   phone's screen, or connect a compatible monitor and open it there, including
   insid
