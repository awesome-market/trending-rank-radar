 What is this?

A curated collection of prompts for AI chat models. Originally created for ChatGPT, these prompts work great with any modern AI assistant.

| Browse Prompts | Data Formats |
|----------------|--------------|
| prompts.chat | prompts.csv |
| PROMPTS.md | Hugging Face Dataset |

Want to contribute? Add prompts at prompts.chat/prompts/new — they sync here automatically.

---

 🤖 Bots & Bot Teams

prompts.chat also hosts always-on AI agents and the teams they work in.

- Bots — Share and discover bots (OpenClaw, Hermes Agent, Grok Bot, and more). Each bot page has install instructions per platform, the computer it needs (cloud, the user's computer, or both), "Better together with" pairings, upvotes, comments, and change requests.
- Bot Teams — Groups of bots that hand work off to each other, shown as an interactive graph. Upvote a team or install every bot in it at once. Featured teams appear on the homepage, and Teams is in the top navigation.
- Profiles — User profiles have Bots and Teams tabs with counts.
- Moderation — Bots and teams can be unlisted to hide them from public listings.

Teams are also available as JSON: GET /api/teams?sort=newest (also oldest or upvotes, paginated with page and perPage).

 📖 The Interactive Book of Prompting

Learn prompt engineering with our free, interactive guide — 31+ chapters covering everything from basics to advanced techniques like chain-of-thought reasoning, few-shot learning, and AI agents. Newer chapters cover going from prompt to team (prompt, skill, agent, bot, team), always-on bots, agent teams, computer use, who may give a bot work, and software development with AI (vibe coding vs. agentic engineering, effort settings, autonomous coding agents) — available in 17 languages.
