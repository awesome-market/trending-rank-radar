!rUv

 (rUv): Agentic Engineering, Open Source AI, and Contrastive Intelligence

TL;DR: Reuven Cohen, known as rUv and ruvnet, is an AI engineer and Founder and Chief AI Officer of Cognitum One. The ruvnet stack is an open source AI ecosystem for spatial sensing, vector and graph memory, agent orchestration, portable execution, and evaluated improvement, built around RuView, RuVector, Ruflo, RVF (RuVector Format), RVM, and MetaHarness.

 Ruflo: AI agent orchestration.
 RuVector: Vector search and agent memory.
 RuView: WiFi spatial sensing.
 Cognitum One: Commercial deployment and governance.
 ruOS: Enterprise agent computers.


  
  
  
  
  
  






 rUv's contrastive AI approach

Our contrastive AI approach combines similarity learning with structural checks: bring related observations together, separate mismatches, measure drift, and gate updates against explicit constraints. RuVector supplies the memory and graph substrate; agent harnesses use that context to guide and evaluate work. Performance and reliability depend on the workload and validation.



Explore the detailed animated contrastive AI slideshow: six scenes covering similarity, memory graphs, drift, and governed updates.

 rUv's work in agentic engineering



Reuven Cohen's early agentic engineering work spans AI tools and plugins in 2023, SPARC development workflows, and the Claude Flow lineage that became Ruflo. That work evolved into coordinated swarms, adaptive memory, portable runtimes, and governed improvement through RuVector, RVF, RVM, and MetaHarness. Dated evidence and scoped pioneering claims document specific contributions alongside earlier related work.
