 Marker

Marker converts documents to markdown, JSON, chunks, and HTML quickly and accurately.

- Converts PDF, image, PPTX, DOCX, XLSX, HTML, EPUB files in all languages
- Formats tables, forms, equations, inline math, links, references, and code blocks
- Extracts and saves images
- Removes headers/footers/other artifacts
- Extensible with your own formatting and logic
- Optionally boost accuracy with LLMs (and your own prompt)
- Works on GPU, CPU, or MPS

 Performance



Marker is built around a simple premise: you shouldn't have to choose between VLM-level accuracy and production throughput, or maintain separate tools for GPU and CPU.

On OmniParseBench (GitHub) — 16,288 pass/fail unit tests across 2,937 pages in 92 languages covering text & reading order (8,499 tests), table cell structure (5,386 tests), and block layout (2,403 tests) — as well as olmOCR-bench, Marker defines the Pareto frontier across every hardware tier:

- Faster and more accurate than open document pipelines (MinerU, docling, PP-StructureV3):
  - Marker balanced (80.5 OmniParseBench, 85.6 on the olmOCR-bench suites, 16.9 pg/s) beats docling (67.5 at 2.1 pg/s) by +13.0 points overall (+34.8 points on text & math) at 8× the speed, and runs 31× faster than MinerU 2.5-Pro (0.54 pg/s).
  - Marker vllm (85.9 OmniParseBench, 83.7 olmOCR-bench, 4.5 pg/s) delivers higher text accuracy (83.8 vs 77.4) and higher layout accuracy (95.7 vs 91.7) than MinerU 2.5-Pro at 8.3× the throughput (4.5 vs 0.54 pg/s).
- Outperforms open VLMs 2–12× its size (GLM-OCR, LightOnOCR-3-4B, PaddleOCR-VL-1.6, DeepSeek-OCR-2, olmOCR-2):
  - Full-page VLMs burn GPU compute on every single page — even clean born-digital PDFs. Marker's hybrid balanced mode keeps clean prose on CPU (pdftext + in-process Indra ONNX layout) and only calls the GPU VLM on equations, complex tables, CJK/non-Latin scripts, and scans — outscoring PaddleOCR-VL-1.6 (78.7) and DeepSeek-OCR-2 (75.2, 3.4B) at 14–21× higher throughput.
  - When you want full VLM OCR on every page, Marker vllm (powered by the compact 0.69B Surya 2.5 VLM) scores 85.9, beating 1B–8B open VLMs (LightOnOCR-3-4B 82.9, GLM-OCR 81.5, PaddleOCR-VL-1.6 78.7, DeepSeek-OCR-2 75.2, HunyuanOCR-1.5 64.6, olmOCR-2 42.6).
