 Cua Spaces

Combine your existing computers into one supercomputer. Your computers, shared by your team and its agents.

Cua Spaces is a desktop app that gives your agents full desktops. Each desktop is a Space: a macOS VM built locally on your Mac, or a Linux or Omarchy image. Spaces run on your Mac, on other computers you own, and in your own cloud account (AWS, Google Cloud or Modal), and the app keeps them in your menu bar and notch.

- Teleport. Move a signed-in app, such as Chrome or Slack, into a Space and it opens there still signed in. Your sessions stay in the Cua Keyvault, encrypted on your Mac, and reach a Space only after you approve.
- Multiplayer. You and your agents work on the same desktop, each with your own cursor. Step in to make a choice, then hand the desktop back.
- Agent-ready images. Spaces images ship with cua-spacesd, so agents get processes, files, screenshots and input as soon as a Space starts.

curl -fsSL https://cua.ai/install.sh | sh


On macOS the installer selects the Cua Spaces app by default and adds the cua CLI. You can also download the signed .dmg, or get the .pkg from the Cua Spaces 0.9.4 release.

Spaces is free for individuals. Pro is $18 a month and Teams is $30 per seat a month, available now from 1 seat. See pricing. The app is source-available under FSL-1.1-MIT.
