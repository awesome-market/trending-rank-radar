Free My TV

A fast, ad-free home screen for Google TV, LG and Samsung TVs.

Trending picks from your own apps, the weather at a glance, and a look that follows the sunset.
Open source. Built with Claude Code. One button to undo.

freemytv.io · Install · LG TVs · Samsung TVs · Any TV · Make it yours





 What you get

- No ads. No sponsored rows, banners or autoplaying promos. Not one.
- Your apps, front and center. The streaming apps you actually use, on a glass shelf. Pick them and their order right on the TV, or (on Google TV and Android TV) let the one you opened last come first.
- Trending, not sponsored. Three fresh picks every hour (up to six, in Home Settings) from what's trending this week and the latest releases, only from apps you already have, and from the services you choose. The focused one opens wide and the others show their posters. Movies and Shows get their own tabs, and on Google TV your own Plex library can join in.
- Continue Watching (Google TV and Android TV). Home Settings → Top of the screen puts what you left halfway in your apps on top, with its progress, and OK picks it up in its app. Or a big clock, or the picks.
- Your TMDB key from your phone. Home Settings → TMDB key shows a QR code: scan it and paste the key. On LG and Samsung, type it with the remote.
- Weather and markets, one press away. The hour-by-hour forecast (or the next 5 days), sunrise and sunset, and live crypto prices; on LG and Samsung, also rain in the next two hours, the UV index and the air quality.
- Live TV or your box on HDMI, one press away. Home Settings → Live TV button picks what the first top button opens: Live TV, as before, or one of the TV's HDMI inputs, shown under the name you gave the input on the TV when it has one (or, on Google TV, Android TV and Fire TV, the name of the device on it). Home Settings → Inputs picks which inputs the drawer shows, and in what order: OK shows or hides one, ← → moves it, Live TV included — so a TV with no antenna can take its tile out. On Google TV and Android TV, Settings → Inputs does the same.
- What's playing. Music, a podcast or the radio going on in the background shows on the home screen and the screensaver: the title, the artist and the app, from any app that tells Android what it plays (Home Settings, off until you turn it on).
- The status at a glance, and a mouse (Google TV and Android TV). Home Settings → Status icons puts Wi-Fi or Ethernet, Bluetooth audio and a USB drive at the top of the home screen; off, nothing listens for them. A mouse or an air-mouse remote works too: hover moves the focus, the wheel scrolls, a click picks.
- Light by day, dark by night. It follows your city's real sunset, the way your phone does.
