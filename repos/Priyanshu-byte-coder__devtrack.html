▲ DevTrack

Turn your GitHub into a high score.

One ranking for every developer. Your commits, PRs, reviews and streaks become one score,
one tier and one spot on a global leaderboard, built from the GitHub activity you already have.
Look anyone up, duel your friends, flex it in your README. Free, open source and self-hostable.


















▶ Watch the trailer (0:41, YouTube): score, leaderboard, duels, README badges and more. Or just type any GitHub username at devtrack.live and see where they'd rank. No sign-up needed.

 For players

 Three steps to your high score

1. Press start. Sign in with GitHub. You approve on github.com; DevTrack never sees your password.
2. Get your player card. Your score (0–1000), tier from Bronze to Legend, XP and level, and your spot on the global leaderboard, built from your real commits, PRs and reviews.
3. Flex it. Duel friends, share your card, and drop live badges into your README.

Just curious? Try any username in the score attack, or open anyone's card at devtrack.live/u/. No account needed.
