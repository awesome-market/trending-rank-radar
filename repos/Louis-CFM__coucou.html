README

Coucou

A tiny friend that lives in your Mac's notch — or at the top of your screen on Windows and Linux — and keeps an eye on your AI coding agent sessions. And now on your phone too: iPhone and Android.

Approve permissions, watch your agents work, drop a file, chat with Claude — all without leaving what you're doing. Walk away from your Mac and Mochi follows you to your iPhone: Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, widgets, Siri.

New in 0.4: Coucou is a real Mac app. Hold a key anywhere, speak, let go: your words are pasted where you type, like Wispr Flow — free, open source, and offline with Parakeet. Record your calls without a bot in the meeting and get the transcript, the summary and the to-dos.

 Why

Some studios showed off gorgeous notch companions… and never let anyone use them.
Coucou is the open version. Every line of code is open source under the MIT License: read it, fork it, learn from it. The Coucou name, Mochi and the sounds stay © Louis Raillé (see License).

Meet Mochi: a soft little squircle with big eyes that pops out of your notch, waves hello, follows your cursor with its eyes, gets annoyed when you poke it (and dizzy if you insist), and tells you the moment Claude Code needs you.

 Coucou on the Mac: dictation, notes, one window





Since 0.4, Coucou has its own window and sits in the Dock. On the left, Flow — everything about your voice — and Notch, all the settings of the island. (macOS, GitHub build)

- 🎙️ Flow dictation — hold a key anywhere on the Mac (right ⌥ by default, or fn, right ⌘ or right ⌃), speak, let go: your words are pasted where you type, in any app. Two quick taps for hands-free (or one, with a setting), Esc to cancel, ⌃⌘V to paste the last one again. Your clipboard gets back what it held.
- 💊 The pill — a small see-through pill at the bottom of the screen (or on its left or right edge). Hover it for a toolbar: dictate, record a call, open Coucou. While you speak, Mochi listens and nine dots follow your voice.
- 🦜 Parakeet, offline — run NVIDIA's Parakeet on your Mac's Neural Engine through FluidAudio: more accurate, 25 languages, nothing leaves your Mac. One click to download (about 650 MB), Apple silicon only. Until then the Mac's own recognition is used.
- ✍️ Your words, your way — a dictionary of names Coucou always spells right, replacements, snippets (say a short phrase, paste a whole text), and an optional clean-up by Claude, on your Claude plan through Claude Code or with your own key: as said, natural or polished, per app.
- 📝 Notetaker — click Record a call: Coucou records your microphone and what your Mac plays, no bot joins the call. Then you get the transcript person by person, a summary, the key points and the to-dos, written by Claude with your key or by Apple Intelligence on your Mac.
