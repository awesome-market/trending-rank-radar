 Intro

- Schedule all your social media posts (many AI features)
- Measure your work with analytics.
- Collaborate with other team members to exchange or buy posts.
- Invite your team members to collaborate, comment, and schedule posts.
- Perfect for automation (API) with platforms like N8N, Make.com, Zapier, etc.

 Tech Stack

- Pnpm workspaces (Monorepo)
- NextJS (React)
- NestJS
- Prisma (Default to PostgreSQL)
- Temporal
- Resend (email notifications)

Latest release

Encrypted channel tokens, connect links, Amazon SES & 40+ fixes v2.26.0 2026-11-13
Highlights

This release brings encryption of the channel tokens in the database (opt-in, with a local key or AWS KMS), connect links so the public API or an MCP agent can hand someone a link that adds a channel to your workspace, Amazon SES as an email provider, and a channel checklist in the save and delete dialogs of multi-channel posts. Instagram carousels go up to 20 items and send alt text, and a sweep of billing and provider fixes removes false "may have already published" warnings, duplicate reconnect notifications and several silent billing dead ends.
